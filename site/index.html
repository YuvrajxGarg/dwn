Cloud

Whole folders. Not 2 GB zips.

Download a big folder from the Google Drive website and you get it chopped into zip parts. Dwn copies it file by file, exactly as it's stored, and picks up where it left off if the connection drops.

  • Shared with me, by person. Everyone sharing with you, each with their files.
  • Shared links. Paste a Drive folder someone sent you and browse all of it.
  • Drag and drop. Your Mac and the cloud side by side. Drag either way to copy.

Connecting Google Drive: Google will say Dwn isn't verified yet

Google is still reviewing Dwn. Until it finishes, its sign-in page shows “Google hasn't verified this app”. Dwn is safe to use: it runs only on your computer and never sends your files or sign-in anywhere. To continue:

  1. Click Advanced
  2. Click Go to Dwn (unsafe)
  3. Click Allow
